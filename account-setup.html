Testing Services Available

OMDL offers a comprehensive range of specimen collection and testing methods, including Urine, Hair Follicle, Oral Fluid, Blood, and Breath Alcohol Testing (BAT).

Drug Detection Timeframes

  • Urine Testing: Detects recent use (1 to 5 days).
  • Hair Follicle: Detects history up to 90 days.

Results Reporting

  • Negative Results: Within 24 hours.
  • Non-Negative: Within 72 hours (after MRO review).
