FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Testing Information

Oxygen Medical Diagnostic Lab provides Third-Party Administrator (TPA) and Consortium services for owner-operators, CDL drivers, motor carriers, and DOT-regulated employers.

If your commercial motor vehicle operation falls under FMCSA authority, your program must follow FMCSA drug and alcohol testing rules under 49 CFR Part 382 and DOT testing procedures under 49 CFR Part 40.

C/TPA Support for Owner-Operators and Fleets

A C/TPA helps with the day-to-day administration of DOT testing, including consortium enrollment, random pool participation, selection notices, testing coordination, Clearinghouse support, and compliance records.

Who Needs FMCSA Compliance Support?

  • Owner-operators operating CDL vehicles in commerce
  • Motor carriers with CDL drivers
  • Companies using leased or contract CDL drivers
  • Passenger carriers with CDL-required roles
  • Employers with safety-sensitive transportation positions
  • New carriers preparing DOT compliance records

Required FMCSA Testing Events

Pre-Employment Drug Test

Completed before a driver performs safety-sensitive duties when required by DOT rules.

Random Drug & Alcohol Testing

Drivers are placed in a compliant random pool, with selections managed throughout the year.

Post-Accident Testing

Used after qualifying accidents when FMCSA criteria and timing requirements apply.

Reasonable Suspicion

Based on trained supervisor observations, documented signs, and proper testing procedures.

Return-to-Duty

Required after a violation and completion of the Substance Abuse Professional process.

Follow-Up Testing

Additional testing schedule directed after return-to-duty requirements are met.

What Oxygen Manages

  • DOT consortium enrollment and certificate documentation
  • Random testing pool management and quarterly selections
  • FMCSA Clearinghouse query and support guidance
  • Pre-employment, random, post-accident, RTD, and follow-up coordination
  • Recordkeeping support for DOT compliance files
  • DOT policy and compliance documentation guidance
  • Supervisor reasonable suspicion training records
  • MRO-reviewed lab results when applicable

FMCSA Clearinghouse Responsibilities

The FMCSA Drug & Alcohol Clearinghouse is the federal database used to track DOT drug and alcohol program violations for CDL drivers. Employers must complete required pre-employment queries, annual queries for current drivers, and maintain proof of those actions with their compliance records.

Keep documentation for each required step, including covered drivers, testing events, Clearinghouse queries, random selections, and training records. This page is general information and does not replace official DOT/FMCSA guidance.
