Why It Matters

Why Implement a Drug-Free Workplace Program?

A structured Non-DOT drug-free workplace program protects your business, reduces liability, and creates a safer environment for every employee — whether you're a small business or a large employer.

🛡️

Reduce Liability

Documented testing policies protect your business in the event of an incident or workers' compensation claim.

⚡

Boost Productivity

A drug-free workforce performs better, has fewer absences, and is less prone to on-the-job accidents.

💰

Lower Insurance Costs

Many insurers offer discounts to companies with certified drug-free workplace programs in place.

🔒

Chain of Custody

Our lab-based tests use a federal CCF — ensuring results are legally defensible and court-admissible.

🚐

Mobile & On-Site

We come to you — post-accident, reasonable suspicion, or scheduled random testing at your location.

📊

Random Program Admin

Let us handle your entire random selection program — compliant, documented, and fully managed for you.
